# 🎂 Happy 23rd Birthday Bittu! (With Infinite Love From Bee 🐝)

A dreamy, romantic, playful, and animated interactive birthday web experience created especially for **Tapshi** (nicknamed *Bittu*) by her boyfriend (*Bee*).

---

## ✨ Features & Flow

1. **🔐 Stage 1: The Secret Love Portal (Password Gate)**
   - Secret Passcode: Strictly **`06 07`** (or `0607`).
   - Cute animated Bee 🐝 character with expressive emotions.
   - Hint reveals: *"A special date: 06 07 🤫"*.

2. **🎂 Stage 2: The 3D Cake Page**
   - **Photorealistic 3D Metallic Foil Balloons**: Both `2` and `3` are rendered as photorealistic inflated metallic pink foil balloons with shine highlights and floating bobbing physics.
   - **Seamless 3D Rotating Cake**: Centered without any boxes, cards, or borders, seamlessly blended into the background.
   - **Candle Blowing Mechanic**:
     - Tap *"Blow Out the Candles & Make a Wish! 🎂💨"*.
     - Smoke trails rise up from wicks as celebratory confetti bursts.
     - Cake smoothly dims into the background in a soft light shade.
     - **Big Screen Hug GIF** (`hugs.gif`) appears in front with:
       - Heading: **"Happy Birthday Bittu ❤️"**
       - Button: **"Read Your Birthday Letter 💌 →"**
   - **Corner Decorations**: Stacked gift box on the left and glowing star jar on the right (pure decorative images without popup modals).

3. **💌 Stage 3: The Dedicated Birthday Letter Page**
   - Hug animation preview at the top.
   - Your exact heartfelt letter recounting meeting in college 1st year, living together in Surat, midnight Bullet rides, jalebis, shawarma, waffles, teaching her how to ride the Bullet, and how she became your light in the dark.
   - Button at the bottom: **"A Quick Question for Bittu 😜👉"**.

4. **🥺 Stage 4: Masti / Love Quiz ("Do You Love Me?")**
   - Question: *"Do you love your Bee, Bittu? 🥺👉👈"*
   - The runaway `NO` button dodges away playfully.
   - If clicked:
     > **"SORRY BITTU, YOU CAN'T SAY NO! HE IS YOURS ONLY, YOU HAVE TO DEAL WITH IT! 😜💍"**
   - Clicking `YES`: Confetti, 100,000% love meter, and button: **"Relive Our Memories 📸💖 →"**.

5. **📸 Stage 5: Our Beautiful Moments Together (New Card Layout)**
   - Matches the requested UI from the screenshot:
     - Header: *"Our Beautiful Moments Together"* in pink cursive script font.
     - Subtitle: *"Every moment spent with you has been magical. Let's cherish these precious memories ..."*.
   - 15 curated memory cards in chronological sequence from college 1st year to 4th year, Surat living, midnight Bullet rides, and her 23rd birthday.
   - Rounded white cards, cursive pink titles (*Her Smile Says It All*, *Together Vibes*, *Pretty Soul*, etc.), captions with emojis.
   - Horizontal scrolling carousel with arrow controls and smooth scrollbar.
   - In-app photo uploader so you can customize and upload your real photos anytime!
